Beware the jubjub bird and shun the frumious bandersnatch. He took his vorpal sword in hand. Long time the manxam foe he sought, so rested he by the tum tum tree and stood awhile in thought. And as enoughish thought he stood, the Jabberwock with eyes of flame came whiffling through the tulji wood and burbled as it came, 1212, and through and through the vorpal blade went snicker. He left it dead, and with its head he went galumphing back. And hast thou slain the Jabberwock? Come to my arms, my beamish boy, O Frab just day, Calloo, Calais, he chortled in his joy.
